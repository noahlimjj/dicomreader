Two views and reference lines

With 2 views on (V), the line in each view shows where the other view's current slice cuts it, e.g. the level of an axial cut on the sagittal image. All levels also draws every slice of the other series as a faint numbered line; click a number to go to that slice. Lines are only drawn between series that share the same patient coordinates (same exam).

About measurements

CT and MR distances use the pixel size stored by the scanner (patient plane), shown bottom-right of the image, e.g. 0.280 mm/px. Plain X-rays usually only store the pixel size at the detector, so anatomy measures about 5–15 % larger than it really is. For exact X-ray values use Calibrate in the Measurements panel with an object of known size.

This viewer is for looking at your own images. It is not a medical device; diagnosis should come from your doctor's report.
