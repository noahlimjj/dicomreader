DICOM Reader

Look through CT, MRI, X-ray and ultrasound scans: scroll through slices, adjust brightness, measure.

  • Open a whole folder (e.g. a hospital CD copied to your phone), pick files, or a .zip from your patient portal.
  • You can also drag a folder or files onto this window.
  • Private: your images are read on this device and never uploaded.
  • For viewing your own images. Not a medical device: diagnosis should come from your doctor's report.
