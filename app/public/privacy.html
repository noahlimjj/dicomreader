Privacy policy

DICOM Reader · last updated 16 October 2026

Summary

DICOM Reader does not collect, upload, sell or share any data. Your scans are opened and displayed on your device only.

Medical images

Images you open (DICOM files, folders or ZIP archives) are read by the app on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no server, no account and no cloud storage. When you close the scans or the app, the images are released from memory; the app does not keep copies.

What the app stores on your device

Only display preferences (for example the last tool you used) and calibrations you entered for X-ray measurements. These stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and does not use the camera, microphone or location. If you choose "Take Photo" or "Photo Library" in the system file picker, that picture is only opened in the app.

Sharing

If you use "Save / share this view", the app creates a PNG picture of the current view and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Not a medical device

DICOM Reader is for viewing your own images, for education and reference. It is not intended for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Contact
